# Insurance Claims — Processing & Management System

## 1. Overview
The Insurance Claims Processing System manages the end-to-end lifecycle of insurance claims — from First Notice of Loss (FNOL) through investigation, evaluation, settlement, and closure. The system supports multiple lines of business (auto, property, health, life) and integrates with third-party data providers, repair networks, and payment systems.

## 2. Functional Requirements

### 2.1 First Notice of Loss (FNOL)
- Multi-channel intake: web portal, mobile app, phone (agent-assisted), email, chat.
- Claim type selection: auto (collision, comprehensive, liability), property (fire, theft, water, natural disaster), health (inpatient, outpatient, pharmacy), life (death, critical illness, disability).
- Policy lookup: auto-fetch by policy number, name + DOB, or phone number.
- Incident details: date/time, location (geocoded), description, involved parties, witnesses, police report upload.
- Photo/video upload: in-browser capture or file upload; auto-compression and EXIF validation.
- Claim number: auto-generated on FNOL submission; format CLAIM-YYYY-NNNNNN.
- Duplicate detection: fuzzy match on policy + incident date + incident type to flag potential duplicates at submission.

### 2.2 Claim Triage & Assignment
- Severity scoring: ML-based triage model scores claims on complexity, potential exposure, and fraud risk.
- Auto-adjudication: low-severity, low-value claims (≤ $2,500) eligible for straight-through processing without human review.
- Assignment rules: skill-based routing (adjuster specialty, language, workload); geographical routing (jurisdiction, state license).
- SLA assignment: based on claim type and severity (e.g., auto glass ≤ 24h, property fire ≤ 48h for initial contact).

### 2.3 Investigation & Documentation
- Damage assessment: integrated with photo-estimation AI for auto and property claims.
- Third-party data: auto-pull police reports, weather data, vehicle history (Carfax), property records, medical records (with consent).
- Statement collection: telephonic (transcribed via speech-to-text), written, video.
- Document management: centralized claim file with version control, redaction, and audit trail.
- Field inspection: schedule adjuster/appraiser visit; mobile app with offline capability for field documentation.
- Estimate integration: auto repair shop estimates, contractor bids, medical bill review.

### 2.4 Coverage & Liability Analysis
- Policy validation: coverage effective date, exclusions, limits, deductibles, endorsements.
- Liability determination: comparative negligence assessment; multi-party liability allocation.
- Subrogation potential: flag claims with third-party recovery opportunity.
- Coverage disputes: workflow for coverage opinion letter generation and legal review escalation.
- Reserve setting: initial reserve within 24h of FNOL; updated on material change; actuarial review for high-exposure claims.

### 2.5 Settlement & Payment
- Payment types: claimant payment, vendor payment (repair shop, hospital), lienholder payment.
- Payment methods: ACH/direct deposit, paper check, digital wallet.
- Settlement calculation: damages + medical + lost wages + pain & suffering (where applicable); structured settlement option for large claims ($100k+).
- Approval hierarchy: ≤ $5,000 adjuster authority; ≤ $50,000 supervisor; ≤ $250,000 manager; > $250,000 executive committee.
- Payment tracking: 1099 generation for applicable payments; escheatment workflow for uncashed checks.
- Release management: digital release form with e-signature; payment released on signed release receipt.

### 2.6 Fraud Detection
- Real-time fraud scoring: ML model evaluating 50+ indicators (claim timing, claimant history, network analysis, social media scan).
- Red flags: claim filed within 48h of policy inception, prior claims at same address, inconsistent damage with reported cause.
- SIU referral: automatic referral to Special Investigations Unit when fraud score exceeds threshold.
- Link analysis: identify relationships between claimants, providers, repair shops, and legal representatives.
- Fraud hold: ability to pause claim processing pending investigation; regulatory timeline compliance.

### 2.7 Claim Communication
- Claimant portal: real-time claim status, document upload, message adjuster, payment history.
- Automated notifications: SMS/email on claim creation, status changes, document requests, payment issued.
- Adjuster dashboard: workload view, overdue tasks, upcoming SLAs.
- Provider portal: repair shops and medical providers submit estimates, bills, and status updates.
- Regulatory notifications: state-mandated communication timelines (e.g., acknowledgment within 15 days, decision within 30 days).

## 3. Non-Functional Requirements

### 3.1 Security & Privacy
- HIPAA compliance: PHI encryption at rest and in transit; access logging; minimum necessary access.
- GDPR: data subject access requests, right to erasure, data processing agreement with third parties.
- PCI-DSS: if payment card data collected for premium or deductible payments.
- State insurance data security laws (e.g., NY DFS 23 NYCRR 500): risk assessment, encryption, MFA, audit trail.
- Data masking: SSN, medical record numbers, and financial account numbers masked in UI based on role.

### 3.2 Performance
- FNOL submission: ≤ 3 seconds end-to-end.
- Claim search: ≤ 1 second for 1M+ claim database.
- Document upload: ≤ 10 seconds for 50MB file with progress indicator.
- Fraud score computation: ≤ 2 seconds.
- Payment initiation: ≤ 5 seconds including approval check and fraud re-screening.

### 3.3 Availability & DR
- Claims system uptime: 99.9% (SLA); FNOL intake 99.99%.
- Disaster recovery: RTO 4 hours, RPO 15 minutes.
- Offline FNOL: mobile app supports offline claim filing with sync when connectivity restored.
- Active-active across 2 regions.

### 3.4 Compliance & Audit
- All claim decisions logged with actor, timestamp, and justification.
- Underwriting file retention: 7 years post-claim closure (or per state requirement, whichever is longer).
- SOX controls for reserve setting and payment authorization.
- Fair Claims Settlement Practices regulations compliance per state.

### 3.5 Scalability
- Catastrophe (CAT) surge capacity: 10x normal claim volume during natural disasters.
- Elastic scaling of FNOL intake, document processing, and auto-adjudication.
- Queue depth monitoring with auto-scale triggers.

## 4. Integrations
- Policy Administration System: real-time policy data and coverage verification.
- Billing System: premium status, deductible tracking.
- Payment Hub: claims payments, vendor payments, EFT/ACH processing.
- Document Management: enterprise content management with retention policies.
- Repair Networks: auto (CCC, Mitchell), property (CoreLogic, Xactware).
- Medical Bill Review: Fair Isaac, Mitchell, or equivalent.
- Fraud Detection: FRISS, Shift Technology, or ISO ClaimSearch.
- Weather Data: IBM Weather, AccuWeather for CAT event correlation.
- Communication: Twilio (SMS), SendGrid (email), Genesys (contact center telephony).
- Regulatory: NCCI, ISO, state insurance department electronic reporting.

## 5. Error Handling & Edge Cases
- Policy not found at FNOL: queue for manual research; notify claimant of delay with expected resolution time.
- Duplicate claim detected: soft-block with "A claim may already exist for this incident"; route to supervisor for merge decision.
- Coverage gap: system flags "No active coverage on date of loss"; route to coverage investigation queue; do not auto-deny.
- Claimant deceased mid-process: freeze claim; require estate documentation; route to survivor benefits team.
- Fraud hold expiration: if SIU does not complete investigation within regulatory timeline, auto-escalate to claims manager for override or denial decision.
- Multi-jurisdictional claim: apply rules for each state; flag conflicts for legal review.
- Payment failure: retry 3 times with alternative methods; if all fail, escalate to finance team and notify claimant.
