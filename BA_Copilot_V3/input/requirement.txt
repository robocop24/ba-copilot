Customer Portal — Requirements

Purpose: Replace the legacy order-tracking system with a self-service portal so customers can manage their accounts without calling support.

Actors:
- Customer (primary user)
- Support Agent (assists customers with access issues)

Functional requirements:
1. Registration — a customer can create an account with email and a password that meets a minimum 8-character policy; email must be verified via a one-time link.
2. Login — a registered customer can sign in with email + password; the session times out after 15 minutes of inactivity.
3. View Orders — a customer can view all orders placed in the last 24 months, including status (placed, shipped, delivered).
4. Download Invoices — a customer can download a PDF invoice for any of those orders.

Business rules:
- Customers can only see their own orders and invoices.
- Passwords must be reset via an email link, never shown in plain text.

Non-functional:
- All account and order data must be transmitted over TLS.
- The portal must support 5,000 concurrent users.

Out of scope: payment processing, order placement, admin dashboards.
